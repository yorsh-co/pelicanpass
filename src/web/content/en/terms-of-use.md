This document explains what the app is for, what we expect from you while you
use it, and what you can expect from us.

## What this app is

A web app from **[RAZÃO SOCIAL]**, CNPJ [CNPJ] (Brazilian company registration
number), made for the {eventName} event. Inside it you check in at the entrance,
collect your T-shirt and your snack, see the event schedule, follow your
check-in number and answer the feedback form at the end of the event.

It exists for this event and nothing else. It is not a social network, has no
sign-up of its own and does not keep working after the event ends.

## Who can use it

Anyone registered for the event. Your registration is made in the event's
registration system, by the volunteer team at the door, or by the person in
charge of a partner group, and your name and phone number come from it.

If you are under 18, the person who made your registration and authorized the
use of your data was **your legal guardian**. They are the one who can ask for
that data to be corrected or removed.

Event volunteers also check themselves in. Those who are authorized read your
ticket to hand over your T-shirt and snack, and the door team can look up and
correct your registration. Each person has access only to what they need for
their own role at the event, and may use what they see only for that role.

## Your link and your code are personal

The link you receive by message and the six-character code on your ticket
identify **your** registration. Check-in is done once: after it, the app
recognizes you for as long as the browser stays open. If you close the browser
or switch devices, you just need to enter the code and the phone number again.

For that reason, do not pass your link or your code to anyone else. Whoever has
them can come in in your place and use what is yours — including the T-shirt
and the snack.

The ticket shown on your screen changes every few seconds, so a screenshot or
photo of it is no use when collecting items.

> If you think someone used your ticket, find the staff at the entrance. The
> organizers will sort it out at the door, with the registration list at hand.

## How to use the app

The deal is simple: use the app for what it was made for.

- Do not try to access another participant's data.
- Do not try to get around the check-in, enter screens that are not yours or use
  someone else's ticket.
- Do not try to copy or reproduce another person's ticket.
- Do not automate access, or try to overload the app.

> The app limits the number of requests per person and uses an anti-robot
> check. Access that exceeds these limits is blocked temporarily, even without
> bad intent — if this happens to you, wait a moment or find the staff.

## The nickname you choose

After checking in you can choose a nickname, which changes how your name appears
on your own screen.

Choose a nickname that respects other people and does not pass you off as
someone you are not. The event staff may remove a nickname that does not meet
this.

## When the app is online

The app runs on a local server belonging to the organizer, during the event.
After the event, it remains available only for the feedback form, until the
closing time announced by the organizers, and is shut down within 24 hours.

Like any system that depends on network and power on site, it may be
unavailable for a while during the event.

> Your entry never depends on the app. If it is offline, or if you have asked
> for your data to be removed, check-in is done manually at the door.

## If you need help

During the event, talk to the staff or the volunteers on site — it is the
fastest way to resolve any access problem.

For personal data matters, the official channel is the data protection officer
of [RAZÃO SOCIAL]: [CONTATO].

## Privacy

The app processes only three pieces of personal data and destroys its database
along with the server after the event. What is collected, what for and for how
long is in the app's [Privacy Notice](/privacy).

## Conference content

The name of the event, the name and brand of [RAZÃO SOCIAL] and the material
shown in the app belong to the organizer. You may use them to take part in the
event, and not to reproduce or distribute them on your own.
